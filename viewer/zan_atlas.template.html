What the levels mean

Arteries and veins (diameter = mean tube diameter measured on the model's mesh): Main 6 mm and larger, plus the named trunks (aorta, venae cavae, carotid, subclavian, iliac, femoral, popliteal, brachial ...); + secondary 3 to 6 mm; + tertiary 1.5 to 3 mm; All also those under 1.5 mm.

Nerves: Main trunks 4 mm and larger, plus the named trunks (sciatic, femoral, median, ulnar, radial, tibial, plexus trunks, cranial nerves ...); + branches 2 to 4 mm; + small 1 to 2 mm; All also under 1 mm.

The minimum diameter slider cuts at any size on top of the level. Main trunks are labelled first and keep a share of the label budget; the label sits on the longest visible stretch of the structure. Only labelled when visible; selected, hovered and marked ones always are. "Hide vessel / nerve labels" turns all of them off.

Abbreviations

· RF-R = base code + side (R / L). Upright = established abbreviation, italic = generated for this atlas. Click a row to select the structure. Arteries, veins and nerves also show their calibre (mean diameter measured on the mesh) and level 1 = main trunk to 4 = smallest; the Labels panel chooses which levels are labelled (help there).

Controls

  • Rotate — drag with the left mouse button, or one finger.
  • Move (pan) — drag with the middle or right button, hold Shift while dragging, drag with two fingers, or press move to make plain dragging pan.
  • Zoom — scroll wheel, or pinch. Shift+wheel pans vertically.
  • Cut — press cut (top right): pick sagittal / axial / coronal / facing me, or place on model and click the model to put the plane through that point; choose which side is removed (or flip side); move the plane along its normal with the slider (millimetres), the −10 / −1 / +1 / +10 buttons, the arrow keys (Shift or PageUp/PageDown = 10 mm) or the mouse wheel over the slider. reset cut restores the default plane, exit cut shows the whole model.
  • Isolate a 3D region — select a structure, press isolate 3D (or key i); everything outside the box around it disappears. The size slider grows or shrinks the box; clear isolation ends it.
  • Hide / fade one structure — select it, then hide or fade in its card, or keys h and f.
  • Solo a layer — the small solo button beside any tissue layer shows that layer alone. Press again to restore.
  • Esc clears the selection; r resets the camera.

Deep structures — brain and spinal cord, nerves, vessels, viscera, cartilage — sit inside bone and muscle, so at full opacity they are hidden behind the surface. Use a preset (nervous, vessels, organs, x-ray), a layer solo, or the cut (top right) to reach them.

Corrections applied

Where this project's own audit of a Z-Anatomy pathway found a real, cited discrepancy against the literature, the geometry is warped by a small, bounded, declarative correction — never a hand-edit of the mesh. A corrected structure carries a badge in its info card; the correction itself is fully described and cited under data/corrections/zanatomy/:

  • Posterior interosseous nerve (both sides) — arcade-of-Frohse entry point moved from the measured 27.5 mm to 42.55 mm distal to the lateral epicondyle, per Aggarwal et al. 2026 (PMID 42556533) and Hazani et al. 2008 (PMID 18668182). See data/corrections/zanatomy/radial_n.json and PROJECT_STATE.md Q144/Q145.

Biomechanical data

Where a Z-Anatomy structure matches one of this project's own atlas entities, the info panel shows that entity's independently-cited facts — TA name, origin/insertion, innervation, functional-compartment PCSA/fascicle length/pennation/force, documented actions — from this project's own atlas records (data/), never invented here. This is the anatomy layer only: the owner's private clinical layer (trigger points, special tests, referred-pain compilations) is a separate, non‑CC‑BY‑SA part of this project and is never included in this file.

A structure badged part of is its own separate mesh — never merged with anything else — that this project's own registry keeps as one coarser entity (a numbered rib, a named carpal bone, one head of a multi-headed muscle); its info panel shows that coarser entity's own cited facts, labelled as the parent's, not independently verified for this specific piece. Structures with neither badge are geometry only — a real Z-Anatomy structure with no match, direct or parent, to one of this project's own entities.

Still approximate

  • Pose is Z-Anatomy's own neutral stance, not driven by this atlas's own joint ROM data.
  • Decimation softens fine surface detail (small foramina, sharp crests) to keep this one page under 15 MB.
  • Name matching between the two datasets is scored, not exact — a few real correspondences are missed, and a few low-confidence ones are deliberately left unmatched (see scripts/zanatomy/map_names.py).
  • Generic body: this is Z-Anatomy's own internally-consistent model, not registered to either of this project's own Visible Human specimens — that registration was tried and found too inaccurate to ship (PROJECT_STATE.md Q142/Q143).
