NMSK Atlas

Six whole-body viewers in three kinds, kept apart. A base model is Z-Anatomy, the textbook body, with nothing fitted to a person. A reconstructed model is rebuilt from the Visible Human CT and cryosections, one real body each; where the scan has nothing, Z-Anatomy fills the gap and is badged as a fill. An adapted model is the Z-Anatomy body moved onto one of those reconstructions. Every viewer opens in its own tab and works on a phone.
