NMSK Atlas

Six whole-body viewers in three kinds, kept apart: Base models are Z-Anatomy, the textbook body, with nothing fitted to a person. Imaging-based models are rebuilt from the Visible Human CT and cryosections, one real body each; where the scan has nothing, Z-Anatomy fills the gap and is badged as a fill. Combined models are the Z-Anatomy body moved onto one of those reconstructions. The green card in each row is the latest version of that model; artifacts whose title starts with OLD are superseded. Every viewer opens in its own tab and works on a phone, tablet and computer; two fingers zoom any picture toward the point between them, and a small 1:1 chip takes it back.

A woman's skeleton under the Z-Anatomy body: the Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D body (one male-derived body; no female Z-Anatomy exists) is deformed onto the Visible Human female's skeleton (her own CT: pelvis, trunk, shoulder girdle and limb proportions; standard pose, left / right symmetrised), and every muscle, ligament, vessel, nerve, organ and the skin is carried with the bones it attaches to (origins and insertions stay on the bone; the shapes stay Z-Anatomy's). The uterus, ovaries, tubes, vagina, ligaments and pelvic vessels of the same donor (HuBMAP HRA, CC BY 4.0) are registered onto the new pelvis; the vestibular bulbs, Bartholin's glands, perineal muscles, perineal body and female urethra of another person (Chinese Visible Human, CC BY 4.0) are now registered onto it too (only the clitoris cavernous body still does not fit without a real bone penetration, so it stays out). The target is one woman, not an average, and nothing of hers is measured here: Combined · Female is fitted bone by bone to HER skeleton and pose, with her measured structures where available.
